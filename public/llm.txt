
# Netligent Technologies

> Netligent Technologies LLP is an IT training and placement institute in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. The institute provides practical, industry-oriented training in software development, data analytics, data science, artificial intelligence,AWS, Cloud Computing and cybersecurity.

## About Netligent

Netligent Technologies helps students develop technical skills through practical learning, projects, career preparation, and placement assistance.

- Website: https://www.netligent.com/
- Location: Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India
